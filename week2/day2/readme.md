## 📚 Topics Covered

### 🔹 Current Date and Timestamp Functions

Used functions for retrieving system date and time.

Functions practiced:
- `CURRENT_DATE()`
- `CURRENT_TIMESTAMP()`

Examples:
- Get current system date
- Get current date and time

---

### 🔹 Date Extraction Functions

Used for extracting parts of a date.

Functions:
- `DATE()`
- `YEAR()`
- `MONTH()`
- `DAY()`
- `DAYOFWEEK()`

Examples:
- Extract year
- Extract month
- Extract day
- Find day of week
- Convert datetime to date

---

### 🔹 Date Arithmetic Functions

Used for adding and subtracting dates.

Functions:
- `DATE_ADD()`
- `DATE_SUB()`

Examples:
- Add days to a date
- Subtract days from a date
- Calculate future dates
- Calculate previous dates

---

### 🔹 Date Difference Calculations

Used for interval calculations.

Function:
- `DATEDIFF()`

Examples:
- Difference between two dates
- Days remaining until year end
- Age approximation

---

### 🔹 Date Formatting

Used for presenting dates in custom formats.

Function:
- `DATE_FORMAT()`

Examples:
- Convert date into `dd-MM-yyyy` format
- Reporting-friendly formatting
